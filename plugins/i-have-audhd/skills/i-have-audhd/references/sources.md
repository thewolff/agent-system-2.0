# Sources

Every source cited in [`rationale.md`](rationale.md) and [`comparison.md`](comparison.md), grouped by
what it supports. Each claim was checked against the fetched source.

## Software engineering and ADHD

- Newman, Snay, Endres, Parikh, Begel, "Get Me In The Groove": A Mixed Methods Study on Supporting ADHD Professional Programmers, ICSE 2025. 99 threads (1,658 posts and comments) from r/ADHD_Programmers, plus a survey of 239 ADHD and 254 non-ADHD programmers. Author-hosted PDF: https://andrewbegel.com/papers/newman-icse25.pdf (ACM page: https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1109/ICSE55347.2025.00242)
- Liebel, Langlois, Gama, Challenges, Strengths, and Strategies of Software Engineers with ADHD, arXiv 2312.05029. Interviews with 19 engineers and 4 managers: https://arxiv.org/html/2312.05029v1

## Executive function and externalizing

- Russell Barkley, The Important Role of Executive Functioning and Self-Regulation in ADHD: https://www.russellbarkley.org/factsheets/ADHD_EF_and_SR.pdf

## Interruption and resumption

- Labonté and Vachon, Resuming a Dynamic Task Following Increasingly Long Interruptions, Frontiers in Psychology 2021, PMC8247645. Interruptions of 5, 15, and 30 seconds: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8247645/
- Chris Parnin and Spencer Rugaber, Resumption strategies for interrupted programming tasks, Software Quality Journal 19:5–34 (2011), extended from their ICPC 2009 paper: http://www.chrisparnin.me/pdf/parnin-sqj11.pdf

## Decision paralysis

- Oroian, Nechita, Szalontay, ADHD and Decision Paralysis: Overwhelm in a World of Choices, European Psychiatry 68 (Suppl 1), 2025, PMC12438291. A conference abstract; 50 adults with ADHD: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12438291/

## Demand avoidance and salience

- ADDA, Demand Avoidance and ADHD: https://add.org/demand-avoidance/
- ScienceWorks Health, ADHD Demand Avoidance vs. Defiance: How to Tell Them Apart. A clinic article; cited only for declarative language as a low-demand approach in pathological demand avoidance support: https://www.scienceworkshealth.com/post/demand-avoidance-in-adhd-overwhelm-vs-defiance-and-what-therapy-does-differently
- Simply Psychology, Object Permanence and ADHD: https://www.simplypsychology.org/object-permanence-and-adhd.html

## Autism, AuDHD, and communication

- The Conversation, What is AuDHD? 5 important things to know: https://theconversation.com/what-is-audhd-5-important-things-to-know-when-someone-has-both-autism-and-adhd-233095
- Hours, Recasens, Baleyte, ASD and ADHD Comorbidity: What Are We Talking About?, Frontiers in Psychiatry 2022, PMC8918663: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8918663/
- Autistic Girls Network, AuDHD (source of "ADHD likes NEW, Autism likes the SAME"): https://autisticgirlsnetwork.org/knowledge_base/audhd/
- Vanderbilt Frist Center, AuDHD: The Hidden Dynamics of a Dual Diagnosis: https://www.vanderbilt.edu/autismandinnovation/audhd-the-hidden-dynamics-of-a-dual-diagnosis/
- Communication in Autistic Adults: An Action-Focused Review, PMC12271282: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12271282/
- National Autistic Society, Autism and communication: https://www.autism.org.uk/advice-and-guidance/about-autism/autism-and-communication
- A Distinct Communication Strategies Model of the Double Empathy Problem, arXiv 2602.02562 (a theoretical preprint): https://arxiv.org/html/2602.02562
