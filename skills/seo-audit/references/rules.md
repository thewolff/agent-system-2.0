These are the only rules the skill may emit. `scripts/audit.ts` emits the `mechanical` rows; the agent applies the
`judgment` rows to evidence.json (SKILL.md, step 3). A rule that is not a row here is never reported.

**The gate.** A row is here only because a primary source fetched in this workload states its *requirement*. The
`quote` column is that source's text, verbatim, and it appears in a file under `research/`. The `source URL` is listed in
`sources.md`. The *detection method* may be inferential; `detection-basis` says so when it is. Every research row's
disposition (admitted, dropped or do-not-flag) is in `sources.md`.

**Columns.**
- `layer`: raw-html, rendered, headers, robots, sitemap, structured-data, lab, field, ua-probe, source, site
  (comma-joined when a rule reads two).
- `mode`: `mechanical` (audit.ts) or `judgment` (the agent; the check names its evidence after "Evidence:"). A judgment
  finding is written in two parts: the observation as **CLAIM** with the evidence.json value as receipt, and the
  assessment unlabeled, with its basis in the sentence. Intent is never asserted; spam-policy rows are indicators only.
- `applies-when`: the condition that makes the row apply; the default is "the audited URL is meant to be indexed".
- `intent-effect`: what `--intent noindex=<url>` does to the row for that URL. `observe`: the exclusion is expected, so
  the row becomes an observation. `keep`: the row stays a finding because it defeats the intended exclusion (IDX-002,
  IDX-003).
- `kind`: `finding` (a defect against the sourced requirement, counted in the verdict) or `observation` (a sourced fact
  whose rightness depends on the owner's intent; reported in its own section, not counted, no severity).
- `severity` (findings only): **critical** when the page is unservable or unindexable for a search surface under the
  default intent (final status not 200, noindex in raw HTML or `X-Robots-Tag`, Googlebot or Bingbot disallowed);
  **high** when a search or rich-result surface loses the page or a signal; **medium** and **low** from the research
  suggestion, with any change justified in `detection-basis`.

**Precedence.** `do-not-flag.md` beats every row here and every note in `frameworks.md`.

**X cards.** The TW- rows cite the Internet Archive copies of X's developer docs (S31, S32 in `sources.md`); they stay
low and are reported "as documented at last archive".
