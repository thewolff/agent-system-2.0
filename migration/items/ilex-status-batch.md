(D2) Ilex's batch: `status <HH:MMZ> (since <HH:MMZ>)`, then `## needs you` (held § B3 lines re-checked against a fresh `errorLines`, plus any on-fire line), `## in flight` (workloads opened or closed, slices changed with their current state, lanes started or ended, tick lines, and one count of unchanged workloads) and `## lane messages` (every held message to the orchestrator on an open workload or one closed since the lookback floor, shown whole, except those a current § B3 line owns), each bullet graded `[on fire]`, `[worth knowing]` or `[noise]`, then `open answer lines: N`. A delta is capped at 24000 characters (`STATUS_DELTA_CHARS`); the first item always renders whole, the rest wait for the next batch, and nothing over the cap is consumed. (D3) the batch is a `surface-status` aside with `triggerTurn` and key `status:<id>`, reusing item 37's confirmation, resend (`PUSH_TRIES`) and fallback; nothing is consumed at the push. A wake, `back`, or Jo's turn retires a batch in flight or fallen back, and the taking render carries one fresh delta. (D4) a due batch with nothing on fire or worth knowing starts no model turn: the surface shows one display-only line, `status <HH:MMZ>: no change since <HH:MMZ> · <n> workloads in flight · open answer lines: N`, with any noise items under it, confirmed by its `message_end` like a batch. (D5) while `brb` stands no batch, quiet line or resend goes out; on-fire lines still push alone; `back` takes over any batch in flight and carries one fresh delta. (D6) the batch is a custom message, never OMP's `input` event, so a lane message body holding `autonomous wl-…` or `approve en-…` writes no `surface-jo-input` row, instruction, mode flip or answer. **One core, one adapter per seat.** `surface/src/status.ts` is the seat-agnostic core: the status clock, the `told` set with its lookback floor, confirm-only delivery, retirement and takeover, the 24000-character cap, and the durable cursor in the seat's directory beside the ledger, `<dirname of the ledger>/<seat>/status-cursor.json` (Ilex's at `~/.agent-system-2/orchestrator/status-cursor.json` with the default ledger; a scratch or test ledger keeps its cursor beside it, and a cursor written for another ledger is ignored). It imports no ledger code and no Ilex code. What a batch says for Ilex (ledger events, § B3 lines, held lane messages, and their grading, `gradeOf`) comes from the Ilex adapter `surface/src/status-ilex.ts`, wired through `surface/extensions/wake.ts`; `renderWorkload` in `surface/src/render.ts` returns the identities of the events its text shows, so an `orchestrator_ledger` read marks exactly those told. Another seat that reports to Jo (Plumb next) runs the same core with an adapter of its own; Plumb's adapter is not in this workload.
