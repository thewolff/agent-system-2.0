title: A workload's close archives its scratch root before removing it, and the daily sweep expires the archives
status: building
workload: none
pr: none
---
**Why.** On 2026-10-28 at about 22:17Z the close of workload `wl-mvs2x210k5ifkz` removed its scratch root seconds after the lane's done-because (`en-mvsodoos0xh1rm`) named the review-cost replay's `REPORT.md` inside it. The report and all its evidence were deleted, and the lane's two "keep scratch" messages were held until after the removal. Jo approved replacing the delete with an archive.

**What.** `closeScratchRoot` (`surface/src/close-entry.ts`) now calls `archiveScratchRoot`: the root is written to one `.tar.gz` at `~/agent-scratch/agent-system-2.0/scratch-archive/<UTC YYYY-MM-DD>/<wl>.tar.gz` (a sibling of the scratch base, so no scratch sweep sees it; `<wl>.2.tar.gz` and so on if a name is taken) by `archiveDir` (`surface/src/scratch-archive.ts`). The archive is first written under a `.partial-*` name, listed back with `tar -tzf` and its member count checked against an lstat walk of the tree, the tree checked unchanged (entries, bytes, newest mtime), and the archive hashed. Only then is it linked to its final name. Only a verified archive lets `removeChild` run. The close records `scratch-archived: <wl> <archive> <bytes> <sha256>` and then `scratch-removed: <wl> <root>` on the standing workload. An archive that fails keeps the root and records `scratch-remove-failed: <wl> <root>: not archived (<reason>); the root is kept`. When the done-because names a path inside the root (as written, physically, through `$AS2_SCRATCH` or `~`), the close result says which archive holds it and under which member name, or that the root was kept. The daily sweep (`surface/scripts/scratch-sweep.ts`) archives every root it removes the same way. Under the write lock it checks that the tree is still the one archived, keeps a root whose archive failed (`archive failed`, exit 1), and records `scratch-archived:` under `scratch-sweep`.

**Expiry and disk guard.** The profile config's `scratchArchive:` block (`profile/config.yml`) holds the settings. `expireDays` (default 14): the sweep removes archives older than this, by mtime, and records each as `scratch-archive-expired: <archive> <bytes>`. `requireOffsite` (default false): when true, an old archive expires only once `<archive>.offsite` exists beside it. An offsite copy job writes that marker once its copy is confirmed, and the sweep removes it together with its archive. `minFreeGib` (default 20): the sweep's `scratch-disk:` needs-you line also opens when free space on the scratch volume (statfs) is under this value, and its `scratch-disk-ok:` waits for both bounds. `$AGENT_SYSTEM_SCRATCH_CONFIG` replaces the installed config as the source (tests).

**Revert.** Revert the PR. The close goes back to deleting roots outright. Archives already written stay on disk and are no longer expired, so remove `~/agent-scratch/agent-system-2.0/scratch-archive/` by hand once nothing in it is needed.
