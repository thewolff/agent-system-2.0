Jo's issue 53 (jo-issues #53). The pair-suggest trigger (`migration/items/pair-suggest-trigger.md`) offered pair as a fourth option and a last body line, `pair: <reasons>. <tail>`. The needs-you line cuts a question body at 120 characters, so that last line never showed, and `pair` was only the fourth word of `answer with one word: extend / accept / stop / pair`. Jo saw the offer about 1 time in 14. After a taken pair, `as2 lane wl-…` sat mid-sentence in the receipt with a second by-hand sentence appended, and the workload then showed the generic idle line ending `relaunch, close, or instruct`. Ilex graded that line as nothing. Plan: `/Users/jowolff/agent-scratch/agent-system-2.0/wl-mvb9mw00ycfjeo/PLAN.md` (rev 6).

What changed:
- Storage stays. The raise sites still store `pair` as the question's last option and still write the `pair-offer:` entry, so every parser, number, batch, paste, the forgery refusal and `applyPair` work as before. The question body no longer gets the `pair:` line: it is byte-identical to the raise with no offer.
- Display changes. Every render Jo reads (needs-you, `pending` and its overflow sentence, the ledger render, the `#n takes:` and `(q-… takes:)` refusals) drops `pair` from the options it lists. When the offer is valid it shows as its own line: `pair offer on <workload words> (q-…): <reasons>. Answer pair q-… to stop the lane and drive it yourself (as2 lane wl-…).` Under needs-you it is an unnumbered `pair` line just above its question, on fire whenever its question is. In `pending` it is an indented `  ↳ pair offer: …` line that a pasted block skips.
- The pair receipt ends with `in another terminal:` (prefixed `when the lane has stopped (a few seconds), ` only when a lane stop was requested), then a line that is exactly `as2 lane wl-…`, then `(copied to the clipboard)` or `(not copied: <why>)`. The copy is best effort: macOS, a UI session, no SSH. A failed copy never undoes the answer. The launch trigger no longer appends its own by-hand sentence to that echo.
- Needs-you shows a taken line after a taken pair until a lane starts on the workload again: `you took pair on <workload words> (q-…); its lane has ended. Continue it in another terminal:`, then `as2 lane wl-…` on its own line. It is always on fire and replaces that workload's idle line. The idle line on any user-driven workload now ends `continue it with as2 lane wl-…, close, or instruct`.
- Ilex (`tiers/orchestrator.md` § Status pings): a lane stopped by a pair answer is a ping trigger. A taken pair and the lane it stopped grade *needs you*, never *worth knowing* or *nothing*, with the command on its own line before the closing ask.
- Gate row 1: one named exception to "the orchestrator hands Jo no command". The by-hand `as2 lane wl-…` line may go in plain text or inline code, never inside a shell fence. This is the exception MIGRATION.md item 52 asked for. Row 1 already behaved this way, so the code is unchanged; `gates/test/message-rows.test.ts` pins it. Recorded in SPEC-2.0.md § B16 items 2 and 3, DESIGN-2.0.md row 1 and the C2.1 row, and the comments of `gates/src/rows/r01-turn-shape.ts`.

Not changed: the offer thresholds and caps (`PAIR_CONFIDENCE`, `LONG_STEER_CHARS`, `LONG_STEERS`, `PLAN_REVISES`, `MAX_OFFERS`, `OFFER_GAP_MS`, `capsAllow`), which signals fire and when, the `pair-offer:` and `pair-taken:` grammars, and the lane-tools `pair` refusal. The lane's wake render and the classifier's log line still list the stored `pair` option; Jo reads neither. A question already open at deploy keeps its old `pair:` body line until it is answered.
