Escalation phase 2, wave 1 (Jo: `split` on q-mvg1l5vvo94ow0, `open phase 2` in-mvg1vdxhuwgt8g; plan `/Users/jowolff/agent-scratch/agent-system-2.0/wl-mvg1vs0rhnu3gg/PLAN.md` rev 5, approved with its recommendations on open questions 1, 2 and 5). It builds on phase 1 (`migration/items/escalation-policy.md`, PR #267) and adds the "now" speed of the policy draft's § 1 and the duplicate and security rules of its § 3. Before this change every question waited for the batch window, a lane or Ilex could raise the same question twice or relay a lane's open question, the first active ruling won even when another disagreed, and a failing gate test reached Jo only when no lane held the workload.

What changed:
- **`now` on both question tools** (`gates/src/rulings.ts` `askJo`, `surface/extensions/lane-tools.ts`, `surface/extensions/question.ts`). `lane_raise_question` and `orchestrator_raise_question` take an optional `now`, one of `integrity`, `security`, `incident`, `guard`, `boundary`, `irreversible`, `money`; any other word is refused and nothing is asked. Such a question skips every standing ruling (and a lane's plan question skips auto-start; its review and shape checks still run) and is raised with `now: <q-…> <category>`, a control entry only the `escalation` writer may write (`surface/src/launch.ts`). Its needs-you line opens `NOW (<category>): ` and is on fire (`surface/src/errors.ts`, `surface/src/batch.ts`). The invariant report's `ruling-miss` skips a question with a valid mark.
- **Repeats reuse the open question** (`askJo`). An ask whose workload, slice, body and options, and for Ilex its `about` workload, equal an open question's returns that id as reused and writes nothing: no row, no `question-about:`, no `plan-question:`. The one exception is a repeat carrying `now` for an open question with no mark: it gains its `now:` mark. A question whose options are all model ids is never a repeat.
- **No relays** (`surface/src/orchestrator-question.ts` `relayedQuestion`). `orchestrator_raise_question` refuses a body naming the `q-…` id of another workload's open question, writing nothing.
- **Ruling conflicts** (`rulingOutcome`). Two or more active rulings that decide one question with different answers decide nothing; the question is raised with `ruling-conflict: <q-…> <ruling ids>` by `escalation`.
- **NOW reserved requests** (`gates/src/rows/r06-reserved.ts` `NOW_RESERVED`, `nowReserved`). A waiting reserved request for a guard bypass (`--admin` merge, a push reaching a protected branch) is tagged `NOW (guard): `; one for an irreversible or shared-state rule (destructive SQL, production database, infrastructure teardown, secret rotation, deploy, publish, workflow run, release, remote ref deletion, history rewrite) `NOW (irreversible): `. Both are on fire; every other request waits for the window. `gates/test/r06-reserved.test.ts` fails when a listed rule is renamed.
- **Security red** (`surface/src/test-run.ts`, `surface/scripts/test-runner.ts`). A full-suite run of any verdict but `superseded`, `red-main` included, with a failing test under `gates/test/` makes the runner send a held `security-red:` message to the orchestrator whatever holds the workload. It renders as `security tests red on <wl>: …` before the test-run lines, past a live lane and a train task's routing, and is on fire. The ordinary test-run line is unchanged.
- **Scratch disk on fire.** The daily sweep's `scratch disk:` line is pushed at once.
- Docs: `USER-GUIDE.md` (sections 2 and 4), `SPEC-2.0.md` (§ B3, *Amended 2026-10-20*), `tiers/lane.md` and `tiers/orchestrator.md`. A lane asks Jo before it changes a failing `gates/test/` test (prompt-only).

Not in this item: never-ask retries, the train-error streak, the noon flow-signals fields and the flow-repair intake text (wave 2, waiting on other PRs), and the merge-train merge notice (its own workload).
