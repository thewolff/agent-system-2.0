**Why:** Jo's brief (ledger input `in-mv8wzcsggrzv9c`): *"no compact on wake. Open a normal workload: Ilex wakes with full history, compacts only on need. Drop stale held lines from the wake; log first-turn size to the ledger every wake (warning, no compaction)."* Plain `as2` started a new orchestrator conversation every time, so a restart lost Ilex's history unless the caller named a session file, and the wake listed a held line for every parked workload even when another line already said why it was waiting (30 lines on 2026-10-15T02:46Z). Plan: `~/agent-scratch/agent-system-2.0/wl-mv8wzipdczs2rk/PLAN.md` rev 7, decisions D1–D5.

**What changed.**
- **Resume by ledger pointer (D1).** `bin/as2` marks an Ilex launch with `AGENT_SYSTEM_AS2_LAUNCH=1` (plain `as2`, and `--resume…`, `--session…`, `-c`/`--continue`; never `--session-dir`, `--goal`, `--fork`, `--no-session` or `--from-*`). `surface/extensions/wake.ts` records such a session as `orchestrator-session: <absolute session file> <sessionId>`, an `assumption` entry by the surface on `wl-orchestrator`, on `session_start` and on `session_switch` (`/new`), only when the newest pointer names another file. The new `surface/scripts/orchestrator-session.ts` reads Jo's arguments with omp's own installed `parseArgs` and, when Jo chose no session, picks the newest pointer whose file exists, is a regular file, was started in the repo and holds an assistant message; a dead newer pointer is named on stderr and the previous valid one resumes. No valid pointer: a new conversation and the stderr line `as2: no recorded Ilex conversation; starting a new one (resume one with: as2 --resume=<file>)`. No session folder is scanned. `/new` and `/clear` inside Ilex start over; there is no `as2 --fresh`.
- **Running-Ilex refusal (D1).** A resuming launch first looks for a running as2-started Ilex (`pgrep -f 'omp --cwd <repo> --append-system-prompt'`). On a match it exits 1 without starting omp and prints three stderr lines per pid: who is running (pid, start time, command line), that two Ilex processes on one conversation would corrupt it, and how to stop it (`/exit` in its terminal, or `kill <pid>`).
- **No compaction at wake (D2).** No code calls `ctx.compact()` at wake. OMP's configured auto-compaction threshold is unchanged and stays the only trigger.
- **Held lines (D3).** `surface/src/errors.ts` leaves out the workload-level held line when another line or the tick already carries the workload: user-driven, an open question, a waiting reserved request, the item-68 idle line, or a workload awaiting close. It stays for a never-launched autonomous workload, an unintended lane end, and an intended end with no open slice and nothing receipted. `SPEC-2.0.md` § A3 carries the amendment.
- **First-turn size (D4).** Each wake records one `wake-size:` entry on `wl-orchestrator`: the prompt size of the first reply after the wake with a nonzero size, or `unmeasured` when superseded or when the session ends first. Warning only: never a needs-you line, never a trigger.
- `USER-GUIDE.md` § 4 (*Held line*, *Start, resume and restart Ilex*) and § 6 describe all of the above. New tests: `surface/test/as2-resume.test.ts`, `surface/test/wake-size.test.ts`, with D3 cases in `surface/test/render.test.ts` and `surface/test/fixtures-fable.test.ts`.

**Follow-up after merge: the launch seat (sl-seat).** `~/.omp/agent/seat-launchers/launch-seat --resume ilex` (outside this repo) today passes `--resume=<newest session file by mtime>` to as2. This workload edits it after the merge, once the first live restart has pinned Ilex's file, so that for Ilex `--resume` passes no session flag and as2's pointer chooses; other seats are unchanged. Both `launch-seat` and `test-launch-seat.zsh` are backed up first as `<file>.pre-as2-pointer-<UTC>`, the Phoenix tracing lines stay intact (the diff against the `*.pre-phoenix-20261015T022500Z` copies keeps the same hunks), the ilex resume case of `test-launch-seat.zsh` expects no `--resume=` on the as2 line, and Jo sees the test's full output before the slice closes.

**Live proof (sl-live).** After Jo merges and pulls main into the checkout the profile loads, two real restarts of Ilex. Before restart 1 the lane records Ilex's session file, its last entry id, its last `compaction` entry id, the last prompt size, and the held lines of two parked workloads (one user-driven, one waiting on a question). Restart 1 runs today's `launch-seat --resume ilex` after the lane checks that its newest-file pick is Ilex's file; this pins the pointer. Restart 2 runs the edited launch seat, whose as2 line carries no `--resume=` while as2's own argv carries `--resume=<the pinned file>`. After each: the first appended entry has the recorded last entry as its parent, no `compaction` or `reset_boundary` entry comes before the first post-restart reply, the ledger holds `orchestrator-session:` for that file and a `wake-size: restart … session <same id>` entry, and the wake shows no held line for the two parked workloads. The receipt is the workload's `done-because:`.

**Rollback.** Revert the PR. `orchestrator-session:` and `wake-size:` entries already written stay in the ledger and are read by nothing; plain `as2` starts a new conversation again. If the launch seat was edited, restore it from its `.pre-as2-pointer-<UTC>` backup.
