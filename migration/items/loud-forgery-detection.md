**Built on branch `wl/wl-mvfxw3c4zqfzls`, PR #275.** Workload `wl-mvfxw3c4zqfzls` (production stakes, plan revision 6 at `~/agent-scratch/agent-system-2.0/wl-mvfxw3c4zqfzls/PLAN.md`, approved by Jo). Jo's ruling (`in-mvfxvdxyywhds0`): detection, not prevention, at accident grade. A check flags a Jo-authority row that no Jo channel wrote, and a lane that resolves its own question. A hit alarms loudly and holds the workload until Jo confirms or reverses the row. Jo's typed answers in a by-hand lane must pass.

**Depends on PR #261** (the Jo-only boundary, `migration/items/jo-only-boundary.md`). This PR stacks on it and merges after it. It adds AUTHORITY rows in `ledger/src/authority.ts` and changes none of #261's trigger rules.

**Jo channels.** A Jo channel is a non-agent process that declares itself one in code (never by env) with `declareJoChannel` (`ledger/src/jo-channel.ts`). There are two:

- the surface: the orchestrator's surface session declares `{ kind: "surface" }` when it becomes active (`surface/src/session.ts`). Every row Jo's words write through the surface is marked.
- the launcher: `as2 lane <wl>` in Jo's terminal (`cmdInteractive`, `surface/scripts/lane.ts`) declares `{ kind: "launcher", workload }` before it opens the ledger. It marks only that workload's rows. This covers the by-hand typed answer the launcher writes (kind answer or withdrawal, author `jo`, channel `direct`, stamp `lane-starter`, a question id) and the surface-stamped rows written with it. The relay that writes that answer is wl-mvb9mw00ycfjeo's PR; either PR can merge first, because the declaration covers every connection the process opens.

After the declaration, every connection the process opens gets TEMP triggers that write a mark into `provenance` for each Jo-authority row it inserts: every `instructions` row authored `jo`, and every `entries` row stamped `orchestrator-surface` that opens with a jo-only control prefix. The mark is `lane_instance = 'jo-channel'`, `detail` `surface` or `launcher`, `tbl` and `row_id` the marked row, `at` the row's own `created_at`. No schema change. `declareJoChannel` throws in an agent context on a live ledger. An agent connection there also gets one more trigger: it may not insert a `jo-channel` provenance row.

**What is flagged** (`checkForgery`, `surface/src/forgery.ts`):

- F1, unmarked: a Jo row stamped `orchestrator-surface` (an author-jo instruction, or a jo-only control entry) with no mark on its own row id. This catches the orchestrator model's own bash or script writing as the surface.
- F2, wrong channel: an author-jo `lane-starter` row that is not the by-hand answer shape, or has no `launcher` mark; an author-jo surface-stamped row on channel `direct`; an author-jo row stamped by anything else, except a lane's own row-11 instruction.
- F3, a lane resolves its own question: a question resolved by an instruction stamped with a lane id (`lane-<stem>-<n>`), or by an answer not authored `jo`.
- F4, machinery resolves a question: a withdrawal not authored `jo`. The runner withdrawing its own ceiling question (`lane-runner`) is not flagged.
- F5, wrong writer: a jo-only control entry whose stamp may not write that prefix. Until now `controlEntries` dropped such a row silently.

F1 and F2's mark tests start at a cutover: the earliest `surface` mark and the earliest `launcher` mark in the whole ledger (`Ledger.joChannelCutovers()`). Rows before a kind's first mark are never flagged for lacking that mark, so the deploy does not flood. F3, F4 and F5 need no mark and apply to every row.

**Where it runs.** The train tick runs a pass first, every 300 s, over every open workload and every workload closed within the autoboard window. The launch reserve (`cmdLaunch`, `cmdInteractive`) runs the check before a lane starts. `bun surface/scripts/merge-train.ts stakes --hold-ok` runs it read-only before a train merge. A hit is recorded once per row id, ever: `forgery: hit <row id> <rule>` on the workload, stamped `forgery-check`, or `forgery: hit <wl> <row id> <rule>` on the standing workload when the workload is closed. Nothing is checked at write time.

**The alarm.** A pass or a refused launch with new hits raises one macOS notification (sound Glass) and one spoken line, `as2 forgery alert. <n> rows on <workloads>` (`surface/src/notify.ts`). A flood is one alarm. Each unanswered hit also gets a LOUD needs-you line naming the row, the rule, what it led to (`launch:` attempts and `train-board:` rows that cite it) and the two words. The line stays until Jo answers. Nothing is pushed into a by-hand lane's terminal.

**The hold.** While a workload has a hit Jo has not answered:

- no autonomous launch (`launchable` returns `forgery-hold`), and the tick asks its live autonomous lane to stop (`lane-stop: … requested forgery-hold by forgery-check`).
- no train boarding or landing (`boardLanding`, `landingReady`), and `stakes --hold-ok` answers `hold: forgery <row id> <rule> on <wl>` for the PR's workload and the train's own. A row reversed after its workload closed keeps boarding, landing and `stakes --hold-ok` refused for good (`… reversed after the close`); only `confirm` lifts a closed workload's hit.
- no close (`orchestrator_close_workload`).
- `as2 lane <wl>` by hand is not refused. It prints the hold line at start.

**Jo's words** (`surface/src/forgery-words.ts`, at the surface):

- `confirm <row id>`: the row was Jo's. Writes `forgery-confirmed: <row id> by <in-…>`. The row is never flagged again. When the last hit is confirmed and the hold had stopped an autonomous lane, it also writes `launch-requested:` so the lane comes back.
- A `forgery-confirmed:` or `forgery-reversed:` counts as Jo's answer, for the check, the hold, the launch, the merge train, the close and the words' own "already answered" refusal, only when it is stamped `orchestrator-surface` and carries a `surface` mark on its own row id (`joForgeryAnswer`, `ledger/src/jo-channel.ts`). For a closed workload's answer on the standing workload, the mark is read from the standing workload's provenance. An answer never counts for its own row. Trust does not chain: a marked confirmation of another confirmation clears only that confirmation's hit, not the row it names. There is no pre-cutover exception, because the words ship with the marks. The effective-reversal readers (`reversedRowIds`) still honour any surface-stamped `forgery-reversed:`. The check's own records (its `forgery: hit` rows and its forgery-hold stop request) are never voided by a reversal, so a forged one that names them hides no hit. A forged one is an F1 hit and holds its workload until Jo answers it.
- `reverse <row id>`: the row was not Jo's. Writes `forgery-reversed: <row id> by <in-…>`. Every decision read then skips the row: control entries, `repo:`, `Ledger.instructions`, `parkLane`'s resolving read, and `invariants.ts`'s `reservedView` (as of its time in `--as-of` replay). A reversed answer or withdrawal (not a cancel) is re-asked on the same slice while the slice is open or paused, and the question's role entries (`plan-question:`, `review-limit:`, `review-park:`, `pair-offer:`, `question-about:`) are copied to the new question. The surface entries that name the reversed answer (`plan: approved`, `pair-taken:`, `ceiling: … by`, `review-rounds-extend:`) are reversed with it. A reversed mode flip is undone only when the mode without it differs from the current mode, so later flips stand. A reversed confirmation no longer exempts its target from the check.
- Reversals are read newest first: a reversal that a later reversal names voids nothing, so reversing a forged `forgery-reversed:` restores the row it hid. A compensating mode flip is recorded with `mode-compensation: <flip id> reverses <row id>`, a jo-only prefix. The surface marks it, F1 flags an unmarked one and F5 one from any other writer. The flip is left out of Jo's choices when a later reversal recomputes the mode only if three things hold: the entry is surface-marked, the flip it names is a surface-marked mode-flip, and Jo's marked `forgery-reversed:` names the row the entry says it reverses. Only the four companion forms above are reversed with an answer, matched at their answer-id field; other entries that mention the id are untouched.
- Reverse cannot undo: a cancelled slice (a deferred cancel still fires when the slice's other open question resolves, through the persistent trigger `questions_resolution_settles_slice`, which needs a schema version to change; a LOUD line says so and Jo re-cuts the slice), an answer whose slice is already done or cancelled, anything outside the ledger the row led to (a launch, a lane's commits, a push, spend, a merge), and anything on a closed workload. The receipt names each. Answer side effects that name no answer id are also left standing: a pair answer's own mode flip, a `launch-requested:` written by a ceiling extend or a mode flip, `review-loop-end:`, a ceiling slice the answer completed, train `skip` cancels, and an orchestrator answer's mirror instruction on its subject workload.
- Either word on a row no hit names, or on a hit already answered, is refused.

**Hazards after merge.** An orchestrator started later from a checkout without this change writes unmarked rows; after the surface cutover each one is an F1 hit (confirm clears each). A by-hand answer from a launcher started before this merged, after the launcher cutover, is an F2 hit.

**Not covered (shelved).** Machinery stamps written by a model (`escalation`, `lane-runner`, `lane-starter`, `notice:`, audit G5) and repo scripts that reach them (G7), except where F5 catches a jo-only prefix. Whether a live lane is by hand is read from its own `launch:` attempt (cause `interactive`, stamp `lane-starter`), not from the workload's mode, so a forged mode flip neither spares an autonomous lane the hold stop nor stops a by-hand one; a forged `lane-starter` row could still mislabel the next lane. The LOUD line and the reverse receipt read launch history raw, so a reversal cannot hide a launch. A deliberate forger who calls `declareJoChannel` on purpose, drops TEMP triggers, or brings its own SQLite.
